# Ownership and attribution inventory

MIT is the proposed license for team-owned code. No LICENSE grant is issued in Phase 0 because the copyright holder, contributor rights and any employer restrictions are unconfirmed. The owner must settle these before publication. “MIT-compliant” is an event requirement, not evidence that the team owns everything supplied.

The engineering handoff is attributed in docs/source-review.md. Its PDF, extracted text and page images remain outside Git; redistribution rights have not been established. Repository documentation paraphrases the specification and records decisions rather than republishing the source.

No application dependency or dataset is installed/committed yet. At Phase 1 maintain an inventory of name, exact version/revision, source URL, license, notices, use and reviewer for Next.js, React, Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, pandas, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, PostgreSQL, Docker, gVisor, Caddy, restic, scanners and any evaluation baseline. Review transitive dependencies and base images too. Include model/provider terms separately; an API model listing is not a license conclusion.

All demo/evaluation data must be labeled synthetic or have recorded permission/license. Track seed/provenance and generator author. Keep held-out oracles private until evaluation is finished. Third-party prior-art citations do not imply reproduced results, endorsement, or permission to reuse their assets.
